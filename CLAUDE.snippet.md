# Spoken announcements (AdjutantVoice)

The `adjutantvoice` MCP server can speak aloud on this machine's speakers via `tts_speak`.

- When you finish a task that took more than about a minute, call `tts_speak` with one short sentence saying what was done, or what failed.
- When you stop to wait on me (a question, an approval, or you're blocked), call `tts_speak` with one short sentence saying what you need.
- Skip it for quick back-and-forth replies, and never speak code, file paths, or long text — keep it under about 15 words.
- If the tool is unavailable or errors, carry on silently; it's a convenience, not part of the task.
